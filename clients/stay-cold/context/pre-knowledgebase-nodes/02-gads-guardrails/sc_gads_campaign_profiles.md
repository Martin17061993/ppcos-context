# Stay Cold Google Ads — long-term campaign profiles (clean-ROAS view)

### Brand search overall: 7 % of spend, ~64 % of tracked purchase value

[Stay Cold · Google Ads · campaign profile · as of 2026-07-03]
The four brand search campaigns ("EX | FRA | SEARCH | BRAND", "EX | DE |
SEARCH | BRAND", "EX | USA | SEARCH | BRAND", "EX | SKANDI | SEARCH | BRAND")
are the account's backbone: about 7 % of spend but roughly 64 % of tracked
purchase value (90-day view). Clean ROAS 90-day: FRA 64.2, DE 63.0, USA 58.0,
SKANDI 24.2. All four qualify for do-not-touch protection (clean ROAS ≥ 1.25×
account average and ≥ 3 purchases). Smallest interventions produce the
largest swings on this account — in both directions (measured range −32 % to
+396 % from similar actions on the same day). Caveat: brand search harvests
demand that is already purchase-ready; its high ROAS is structural, and the
marginal return of extra brand spend is far below the average shown.

### Brand search visibility gap: rank-limited, not budget-limited (USA worst)

[Stay Cold · Google Ads · campaign profile · as of 2026-07-03]
Brand impression share (60-day view): FRA 93.3 %, DE 92.4 %, SKANDI 89.5 %,
USA 82.8 %. The lost share is rank-lost (USA: 15.6 % rank-lost IS), not
budget-lost — so the fix is bids and ad quality, not budget. Target
established by the analysis: 95–99 % brand impression share. Evidence that
closing the gap pays: the 2026-03-13 USA brand package (budget 100 → 140
EUR/day plus keyword cleanup plus ad-group tROAS) measured +261 % vs. account
trend. Cheapest lever in the account.

### EX I SHOPPING I FOKUSPRODUKTE — flagship spender with a measured saturation ceiling

[Stay Cold · Google Ads · campaign profile · as of 2026-07-03]
Largest spender: ~97,000 EUR in H1 2026 = 39 % of account spend, H1-2026
clean ROAS 2.32. Demonstrated both halves of the budget rule: scaling in
steps worked in Feb–Apr 2026 while budget-lost impression share was 52–67 %
(a real limit); a single oversized step failed even with a real limit
(2025-04-05: +77 % at 56 % budget-lost IS → −17 % vs. trend); and pushing
spend with no limit failed hard (June 2026: budget-lost IS 0–1 %, weekly
clean ROAS 1.5–1.8, marginal clean ROAS of the June increment 0.63). Budget
cuts on this campaign measured positive twice (2025-03-10: −34 % budget →
+17 %; 2025-08-11: 185 → 136 EUR → +29 %). Structure work also paid
(2026-01-23 ad-group restructuring: +29 %).

### EX I WW I PMAX I SCALING I FEED ONLY I OVER-INDEX — the scarcity showcase

[Stay Cold · Google Ads · campaign profile · as of 2026-07-03]
PMax flagship for feed-only scaling. Both of its high-confidence wins were
scarcity packages: 2024-10-04/09 (tROAS 360 % → 560 %, budget 175 → 76 EUR)
→ +126 % and +246 % vs. trend; 2025-04-15 correction package (budget 115 →
66 EUR, tROAS 450 % → 250 %) → +86 %. A +400 % budget jump (2025-04-04, 23 →
115 EUR) produced no relative effect. History of target thrash: tROAS was
changed 560 → 300 → 450 within November 2024 (unmeasurable, BFCM window) and
again later — target changes in under 14-day intervals destabilized the
campaign and made measurement impossible. Moderate budget step +44 %
(2025-07-14, 66 → 95 EUR) measured +58 % in a calm window.

### EX I WW I PMAX I SCALING I FEED ONLY I PROSPECTING — step size decides

[Stay Cold · Google Ads · campaign profile · as of 2026-07-03]
Prospecting arm of the feed-only PMax setup; chronically zero budget-lost
impression share (never budget-limited). Track record: moderate increase
+44 % in a calm window → +43 % vs. trend (2025-01-23, high confidence);
large jumps always negative: +143 % (2025-04-04) → −23 %, +64 % then revert
within 6 days (2025-07-14/20) → −21 %/+27 % whipsaw, +44 % amid 8 parallel
changes (2025-10-23) → −31 %, +96 % (2026-05-29, 450 → 880 EUR) → −27 % on
~17,400 EUR window spend, the most expensive single decision of H1 2026.

### EX I WW I PMAX I SCALING I BROAD and EX I WW I PMAX I PROSPECTING I BROAD

[Stay Cold · Google Ads · campaign profile · as of 2026-07-03]
"SCALING I BROAD": proved the scarcity lesson early (2024-07-16 cut 330 → 150
EUR → +45 %, high confidence; 2024-10-09 cut 136 → 102 EUR → +31 %, high),
then had the lesson violated twice (2024-08-21 re-raise → −22 %; 2025-10-11
jump 115 → 350 EUR at 8.6 % budget-lost IS → −18 %). "PROSPECTING I BROAD":
pausing this profitable prospecting campaign on 2025-05-13 measured −118 %
vs. trend (high confidence) — the hardest negative verdict in two years;
pausing needs the same evidence bar as spending.

### BS DEALS (Demand Gen) — healthy sale campaign, steered via intraday scaling

[Stay Cold · Google Ads · campaign profile · as of 2026-07-10]
Demand-Gen campaign "BS DEALS - DG_ CP+PDP_S+V", H1-2026 clean ROAS 5.78 —
fundamentally healthy. During the Black Spring Sale 2026 (promo window
2026-03-12 to 2026-03-20) it was steered with deliberate intraday scaling
(bidding switched target-ROAS → target-CPA, budget ×7 up and back within six
days) — per operator review 2026-07-10 an intentional sale tactic. Per-change
verdicts inside promo windows are not meaningful for this campaign; evaluate
its sales as packages (promo vs. previous promo, purchase-only series). Keep
its documented promo windows in the promo calendar so analyses treat them
correctly.
