# Stay Cold — Ad Copy Rules

> NEW (2026-07-21), for the future ad-copy agent and any human writing RSAs/assets for this
> account. Sources: 01 Brand Cheat Sheet v0.1 (Active), 03 Product + Offer Cheat Sheet v0.1
> (Active), 04 Marketing Cheat Sheet v0.1 (Active), 02 Creative Cheat Sheet v0.2 (Draft),
> Klaviyo drop SOP (tone reference). **All copy is written in English.**

## Brand Voice

- Dark, terse, self-confident. The brand "sets a standard", it does not persuade: "We don't explain ourselves. We don't advertise."
- Ads must read like brand communication, not like advertising. No hype punctuation, no clickbait.
- Slogan: "Defeat Conformity. Stay Cold." — usable as an asset.
- Audience: communicate ONLY to the core (metal, tattoo, hardcore, dark streetwear). Never soften copy to chase the mainstream-adjacent audience.

## Hard No-Gos (locked brand language rules)

1. **Never say "Premium Quality".**
2. **Never say "Community".**
3. **Never say "Streetwear".**
4. No emojis.
5. No discount screaming — the documented negative example is "Only today 50%!!!". No sale framing at all outside Black Friday slow-mover scope (locked offer rule: no sitewide discounts; no discounts on new drops < 90 days).
6. No fake urgency. Honest scarcity only: daily drops = "one day", Friday deals = "one week", real sell-out risk only when stock data supports it.
7. Symbolism: dark energy yes (skulls, dark graphics) — "use the energy, not the religion". No anti-religious provocation. Creative direction is moving toward "powerful, positive symbolism" (02 draft) — do not push new occult/gore motifs in copy.
8. Never make the price look negotiable (retention runs on identity, not incentives).

Every other product claim must be verified against live data (price, stock, product attributes) before use — same guardrail as Stay Cold's own Klaviyo SOP.

## Structural Rules for RSAs

- Headlines: lead with identity/product truth, not offers. Brand campaigns: protect exact brand terms; never pin generic offer headlines into brand RSAs.
- Descriptions: material/durability facts + drop mechanics beat adjectives.
- Display paths: product category or drop name, in brand tone (e.g. /heavyweight, /reign-of-blood). No /sale outside BF scope.
- CTAs: restrained. "Shop the drop" style; no "Buy now!!" urgency stacking.
- A/B discipline: one variable per test; respect the account guardrails on change staggering (no same-day rollouts across all market campaigns — DON'T-9).

## Interaction with Account Guardrails

- The 4 brand search campaigns are do-not-touch (DON'T-4): the ad-copy agent proposes NO asset changes there without explicit human instruction; RSA replace-pushes into brand campaigns are out of scope by default.
- Push mechanics: replace-pushes clone the RSA with the swapped asset and pause the old one (max 5 replaces per push) — high-impact; approval gate mandatory.
- Naming reference for campaigns/sendouts at Stay Cold: `EX I <THEME> I DDMMYYYY` (their e-mail convention; keep ad naming consistent where applicable).
