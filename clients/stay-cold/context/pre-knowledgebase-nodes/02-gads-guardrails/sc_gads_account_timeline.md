# Stay Cold Google Ads — account timeline and monthly series (clean ROAS)

### Account story 2024–2026 in five phases

[Stay Cold · Google Ads · timeline · as of 2026-07-03]
Measured on the purchase-only series ("clean ROAS"): (1) Baseline Jul–Sep 2024:
24–32k EUR spend per month at clean ROAS 5.2–5.6 with a broad PMax/Shopping
setup. (2) Efficiency program Oct 2024: budgets cut and tROAS targets raised
on the biggest PMax campaigns — clean ROAS jumped 5.45 → 8.01 → 11.93
(Sep→Oct→Nov). (3) Efficiency plateau Nov 2024 – Oct 2025: disciplined spend
of 16–33k, clean ROAS 9–14, best months Aug/Sep 2025 (12.78 / 14.24).
(4) BFCM Nov 2025: 34k spend at clean ROAS 19.38, +62 % purchase revenue vs.
BFCM 2024 — a real, measurement-clean success. (5) Scaling Jan–Jun 2026:
spend tripled 21k → 62k while clean ROAS fell six months in a row,
11.63 → 5.04.

### Year-over-year anchors (measurement-clean)

[Stay Cold · Google Ads · timeline · as of 2026-07-03]
H2 2025 vs. H2 2024: spend roughly flat (~152k vs. ~158k EUR), purchase
revenue +66 % (about 2.00M vs. 1.21M EUR), clean ROAS 13.2 vs. 7.6 — a real,
season-adjusted improvement, driven by the Oct 2024 efficiency program and a
strong BFCM. H1 2026 vs. H1 2025: spend +92 % (~250k vs. ~130k), purchase
revenue +48 %, clean ROAS 7.79 vs. 10.08 (−23 %). The marginal clean ROAS of
the additional H1-2026 spend was about 5.3 over the half year — defensible on
average, but it collapsed in June 2026 (marginal 0.63 on the biggest spender).

### Current state June 2026 (analysis cutoff)

[Stay Cold · Google Ads · timeline · as of 2026-07-03]
June 2026: 61,961 EUR spend, clean ROAS 5.04 (account surface shows 7.85).
The account is past its saturation point on the main Shopping campaign
(EX I SHOPPING I FOKUSPRODUKTE: budget-lost impression share ≈ 0–1 %, marginal
clean ROAS of the June budget increment 0.63). A first corrective budget cut
on that campaign happened 2026-06-30 (1,600 → 550 EUR/day). Whether non-brand
activity at clean ROAS ~2.4 (90-day view) is profitable depends on the DB1
margin, which is not available in the account.
