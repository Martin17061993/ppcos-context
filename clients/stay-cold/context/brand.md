# Brand Context - Stay Cold Apparel

## Summary

Stay Cold Apparel is a Berlin-based (MASCANI GmbH) D2C tattoo-art streetwear brand, 10+ years in business, selling oversized heavyweight tees, hoodies, pants, shorts and accessories designed in collaboration with tattoo artists and illustrators worldwide. Positioning is anti-conformist, dark/death-metal-adjacent, art-led — "limited art pieces" rather than blank basics. Sells worldwide in 90+ currencies from a German warehouse, with a drop-driven merchandising rhythm ("Thunder Drop Deal", "New Drops", named collections) and 6,473 REVIEWS.io reviews at 4.6/5.

## Tone of Voice

Aggressive, profane-adjacent, unapologetic subculture voice. Written like a metal/tattoo scene insider talking to peers, not like a retailer talking to shoppers. Heavy use of imperatives, all-caps, mythology and horror imagery, and deliberate anti-marketing phrasing. Product copy is narrative — each design gets a story and an artist credit — rather than spec-first.

**Characteristics:**
- Confrontational and identity-driven ("rebels", "defeat conformity", "individualists")
- Slangy and unpolished on purpose ("the illest", "ain't afraid", "this messed up world", "The Shit Right Now")
- Mythological / occult / death-metal imagery (Oni Kijo, Reign Of Blood, Shadow Division, Vlad The Impaler)
- Artist-first credibility — designers named on every product
- Quality claims stated bluntly, not with luxury language ("quality over everything", GSM weights in every product title)

## Target Audience

**Type:** B2C

**Demographics:**
Not stated on-site. Implied: 18–35, heavily male-skewing but explicitly unisex (XS–3XL, crop tops in range), Europe-first (Germany/EU is the fastest-shipping and default-currency market) with meaningful US/UK/global tail. Tattooed or tattoo-adjacent, metal/hardcore/alt-music culture, skate/street culture, horror and occult aesthetics.

**Pain Points Addressed:**
- Mainstream streetwear feels generic — "don't settle for basic"
- Wanting clothing that signals identity and subculture membership, not just fits
- Cheap graphic tees that fade, thin fabric, poor print durability → answered with explicit GSM weights (180–400GSM), 100% cotton, screen-print, care instructions
- Not finding real tattoo art on garments — "which you simply can't find anywhere else"

**Customer Personas:**
- The tattooed scene regular — follows specific tattoo artists, buys to support/wear their art (artist filter on collection pages is a first-class navigation facet)
- The metal / hardcore fan — death-metal aesthetic, band-merch buying habits, responds to drops and scarcity
- The oversized-fit streetwear buyer — cares about 350/400GSM heavy fabric and drape, cross-shops heavyweight streetwear labels
- The collector — buys limited drops, restocks, and collabs; motivated by "sold out" and "limited restock" signals

## Google Ads Implications

**Strong ad-copy assets:**
- 4.6/5 from 6,473 reviews (seller ratings + review extension eligible)
- 10+ years in business
- Free shipping from €89 · 30-day returns
- Named tattoo artists (strong differentiator vs. generic streetwear competitors)
- GSM weight as a hard quality proof point

**Risks / flags to check before writing ads:**
1. **"Worldwide Delivery within ~1 - 2 business days" is a dispatch claim, not a delivery claim** — the FAQ says USA 10–14 days, Australia 4–6 weeks. Do NOT put a 1–2 day *delivery* promise in ad copy for non-DE geos. Misleading-claim risk under Google Ads policy and a direct CVR/return-rate risk.
2. **Even the dispatch claim is inconsistent internally** — FAQ says 2–3 business days, product page says 24 hours, banner says 1–2 days. Needs a single source of truth before it goes into RSAs or promotion extensions.
3. **`context/business.md` is configured for a lead-gen business** (Form Submit / Phone Call / Chat conversions, $150 value per lead, 20% lead-to-close, $2,500 deal value, USD targets) — this is a EUR ecommerce account. Targets and conversion economics in business.md do not match this brand and will produce wrong recommendations until corrected.
4. High sold-out rate across collections — check whether Shopping/PMax feed is excluding out-of-stock items and whether Search ads point at live LPs.
