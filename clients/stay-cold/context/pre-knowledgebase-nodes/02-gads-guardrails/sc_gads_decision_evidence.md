# Stay Cold Google Ads — dated decision evidence (what was changed, what happened)

Method note for every entry: effect = campaign performance 28 days after vs.
28 days before the change, minus the whole account's change in the same window
("DiD", removes season and account trend), measured on purchase-only revenue.
Attribution, not proof. Confidence "high" = |DiD| ≥ 30 %, ≤ 3 parallel
changes, no counting-regime break in the window.

## Positive evidence

### 2024-10-09 · PMax OVER-INDEX · budget cut 175 → 76 EUR/day → +246 % (high)

[Stay Cold · Google Ads · decision evidence · as of 2026-07-03]
On 2024-10-09 the daily budget of campaign "EX I WW I PMAX I SCALING I FEED
ONLY I OVER-INDEX + INDEX + NEAR-INDEX" was cut from 175 to 76 EUR. Effect:
+246 % vs. account trend (high confidence, ~5,270 EUR spend in window). Part
of the October 2024 efficiency program. Why it worked: with a loose budget,
smart bidding was buying unprofitable marginal auctions; scarcity forced it
to drop the weakest traffic first. Strongest single proof on this account
that efficiency lived in the existing spend, not in buying more.

### 2024-10-04 · PMax OVER-INDEX · tROAS target 360 % → 560 % → +126 % (high)

[Stay Cold · Google Ads · decision evidence · as of 2026-07-03]
On 2024-10-04 the target ROAS of campaign "EX I WW I PMAX I SCALING I FEED
ONLY I OVER-INDEX + INDEX + NEAR-INDEX" was raised from 360 % to 560 %.
Effect: +126 % vs. account trend (high confidence). Together with the budget
cut of 2024-10-09 this formed the efficiency package that lifted account
clean ROAS from 5.45 (Sep) to 8.01 (Oct) to 11.93 (Nov 2024), an effect that
persisted into spring 2025 (Mar 2025: 11.10 vs. Mar 2024: 7.05 — so not
seasonality). After the package the campaign was left alone for 14+ days,
which is part of why the effect is cleanly measurable.

### 2024-07-16 · PMax SCALING BROAD · budget cut 330 → 150 EUR/day → +45 % (high)

[Stay Cold · Google Ads · decision evidence · as of 2026-07-03]
On 2024-07-16 the daily budget of campaign "EX I WW I PMAX I SCALING I BROAD"
was cut by 55 %, from 330 to 150 EUR. Effect: +45 % vs. account trend (high
confidence, ~9,185 EUR spend in window — the largest volume among the
positive evidence). Earliest proof of the scarcity pattern on this account,
three months before the October 2024 efficiency program repeated it.

### 2026-03-13 to 2026-03-18 · Search USA Brand · care package → +261 % (medium)

[Stay Cold · Google Ads · decision evidence · as of 2026-07-03]
On 2026-03-13 campaign "EX | USA | SEARCH | BRAND" received a package: daily
budget 100 → 140 EUR, broad-match keyword "stay cold shop" removed and a
phrase-match variant added, and an ad-group-level tROAS set. Effect: +261 %
vs. account trend (medium confidence). Why: USA brand was the undersupplied
brand campaign (impression share 82.8 %, rank-lost impression share 15.6 %) —
the package closed a visibility gap with almost no extra media cost. Two cautions:
(1) an automated "auto-apply" removal of the exact keyword [stay cold] on
2026-03-18 sits inside the same measurement window and shows "+250 %" — a
free rider of the package, not evidence that auto-apply removals are good;
(2) the package itself sits inside the Black Spring Sale window (2026-03-12
to 2026-03-20) — the +261 % is account-trend-corrected, which absorbs most of
the sale lift, but promo mix effects cannot be fully excluded.

### 2024-09-23 · Search SKANDI Brand · broad keyword paused → +278 % (high)

[Stay Cold · Google Ads · decision evidence · as of 2026-07-03]
On 2024-09-23 the broad-match keyword "stay cold hoodie" was paused in
campaign "EX | SKANDI | SEARCH | BRAND". Effect: +278 % vs. account trend
(high confidence). Why: broad match inside a brand campaign leaked spend into
generic apparel auctions. Lesson encoded in the guardrails: broad match has
no place in brand campaigns of this account.

### 2026-01-13 · Brand promo-asset cleanup · SKANDI +396 % (high) but USA −32 % (high)

[Stay Cold · Google Ads · decision evidence · as of 2026-07-03]
On 2026-01-13, after the holiday sale period, expired promotion assets were
paused across brand campaigns. Same action, opposite outcomes by market:
"EX | SKANDI | SEARCH | BRAND" +396 % vs. account trend (high confidence),
"EX | USA | SEARCH | BRAND" −32 % (high confidence), a third brand campaign
−19 % (medium). Lesson: even a plausible cleanup acts differently per market;
roll changes out market by market, staggered, never as a same-day batch
across all brand campaigns.

### 2025-04-15 · PMax OVER-INDEX · correction package → +86 % (high)

[Stay Cold · Google Ads · decision evidence · as of 2026-07-03]
On 2025-04-15 campaign "EX I WW I PMAX I SCALING I FEED ONLY I OVER-INDEX +
INDEX + NEAR-INDEX" got a correction package: daily budget 115 → 66 EUR and
tROAS target 450 % → 250 %. Effect: +86 % vs. account trend (high
confidence). Context: eleven days earlier (2025-04-04) the same campaign's
budget had been jumped from 23 to 115 EUR (+400 %), which produced no
relative effect (−1 %, neutral). The correction — shrink budget, reset a
digestible target, then 14+ days of rest — is the same scarcity pattern as
October 2024.

### 2026-01-23 · Shopping Fokusprodukte · ad-group restructuring → +29 % (medium)

[Stay Cold · Google Ads · decision evidence · as of 2026-07-03]
On 2026-01-23 campaign "EX I SHOPPING I FOKUSPRODUKTE" was restructured: a
new ad group with income-based targeting was created and another ad group
paused. Effect: +29 % vs. account trend (medium confidence, ~6,500 EUR spend
in window) at a time when the campaign had no budget limit (budget-lost
impression share ≈ 0). This was the last clearly positive intervention on
the campaign before the 2026 saturation phase; structure beat budget.

### 2026-03-06 · Brand campaigns · targeted tracking-template repair → +66/+25/+17 % (medium)

[Stay Cold · Google Ads · decision evidence · as of 2026-07-03]
On 2026-03-06 the tracking templates of three brand search campaigns
(including "EX | USA | SEARCH | BRAND") were repaired in a targeted pass.
Effects: +66 %, +25 %, +17 % vs. account trend (medium confidence). This is
the disciplined counter-example to the account-wide tracking-template rollout
of 2024-10-23 (which hit 7 of 11 campaigns negatively): same type of change,
but done per campaign and verifiable.

### 2025-08-11 · Shopping Fokusprodukte · budget cut 185 → 136 EUR/day → +29 % (medium)

[Stay Cold · Google Ads · decision evidence · as of 2026-07-03]
On 2025-08-11 the daily budget of campaign "EX I SHOPPING I FOKUSPRODUKTE"
was cut from 185 to 136 EUR while budget-lost impression share was only 12 %.
Effect: +29 % vs. account trend (medium confidence, ~5,760 EUR window spend).
Scarcity worked on the Shopping flagship too — three months before the 2026
scaling wave attempted the opposite on the same campaign.

## Negative evidence

### 2026-05-29 · PMax Feed-Only Prospecting · budget +96 % with zero budget limit → −27 % (medium)

[Stay Cold · Google Ads · decision evidence · as of 2026-07-03]
On 2026-05-29 the daily budget of campaign "EX I WW I PMAX I SCALING I FEED
ONLY I PROSPECTING" was raised from 450 to 880 EUR while budget-lost
impression share was ≈ 0 % (no budget limit existed). Effect: −27 % vs.
account trend (medium confidence) on ~17,400 EUR window spend — the most
expensive single decision of H1 2026. With no budget constraint, the extra
money could only buy more expensive rank traffic.

### 2025-05-13 · PMax Prospecting Broad · profitable campaign paused → −118 % (high)

[Stay Cold · Google Ads · decision evidence · as of 2026-07-03]
On 2025-05-13 campaign "EX I WW I PMAX I PROSPECTING I BROAD" was paused.
Effect: −118 % vs. account trend (high confidence) — the hardest negative
verdict in two years. The pause removed profitable prospecting volume during
the efficiency plateau with no replacement. Lesson: pausing needs the same
evidence bar as increasing; "off" is also a bidding decision.

### 2024-10-23 · account-wide tracking-template rollout → 7 of 11 campaigns negative

[Stay Cold · Google Ads · decision evidence · as of 2026-07-03]
On 2024-10-23 tracking templates were switched account-wide in one day (a
client-side login executed the change, presumably a tracking-tool migration).
Of 11 significantly measurable campaign clusters, 7 were negative (worst:
"EX I SHOPPING I FOKUSPRODUKTE" −47 % vs. account trend), 2 neutral, 2
positive. Part of the measured deltas is measurement damage (attribution
disruption) rather than auction damage — which is exactly the problem: an
unstaggered rollout makes the two indistinguishable. Lesson: roll out
account-wide technical changes stepwise, never in one day.

### 2025-04-04 · PMax Feed-Only Prospecting +143 % (−23 %) and PMax OVER-INDEX +400 % (no effect)

[Stay Cold · Google Ads · decision evidence · as of 2026-07-03]
On 2025-04-04 two large budget jumps happened: campaign "EX I WW I PMAX I
SCALING I FEED ONLY I PROSPECTING" from 115 to 280 EUR/day (+143 %) →
−23 % vs. account trend (medium confidence); campaign "EX I WW I PMAX I
SCALING I FEED ONLY I OVER-INDEX + INDEX + NEAR-INDEX" from 23 to 115 EUR
(+400 %) → no relative effect (−1 %, neutral). Pattern across the whole
account: budget increases above +90 % per step were never positive (0 of 6
cases; 4 negative, 2 neutral) — big jumps reset smart-bidding learning and
buy marginal traffic at best.

### 2025-04-05 · Shopping Fokusprodukte · +77 % budget despite a real budget limit → −17 % (medium)

[Stay Cold · Google Ads · decision evidence · as of 2026-07-03]
On 2025-04-05 the daily budget of campaign "EX I SHOPPING I FOKUSPRODUKTE"
was raised from 175 to 310 EUR (+77 %) while budget-lost impression share was
56 % — a genuine budget limit existed. Effect was still −17 % vs. account
trend (medium confidence). Lesson: headroom (a real budget limit) is a
necessary condition for raising budget, but not a sufficient one — step size
still matters, because the bidder has to digest the new level.

### 2025-04-17 · Shopping Fokusprodukte · panic revert 310 → 151 EUR → −22 % (medium)

[Stay Cold · Google Ads · decision evidence · as of 2026-07-03]
On 2025-04-17, twelve days after the oversized budget increase of 2025-04-05,
the budget of campaign "EX I SHOPPING I FOKUSPRODUKTE" was cut from 310 to
151 EUR. Effect: −22 % vs. account trend (medium confidence). Whipsaw costs
in both directions: the bidder recalibrated twice within two weeks. A
moderate first step with a 7+ day rest would have avoided both losses.

### 2025-10-11 · PMax Scaling Broad · budget +204 % at 8.6 % budget-lost IS → −18 % (medium)

[Stay Cold · Google Ads · decision evidence · as of 2026-07-03]
On 2025-10-11 the daily budget of campaign "EX I WW I PMAX I SCALING I BROAD"
was raised from 115 to 350 EUR (+204 %) while budget-lost impression share
was 8.6 %. Effect: −18 % vs. account trend (medium confidence). Notable
because the same campaign had proven the opposite lesson a year earlier
(budget cut 2024-07-16: +45 %; cut 2024-10-09: +31 %). Without codified
rules, the account repeated a corrected mistake one year later.

### 2024-08-21 · PMax Scaling Broad · re-raise five weeks after the successful cut → −22 % (medium)

[Stay Cold · Google Ads · decision evidence · as of 2026-07-03]
On 2024-08-21 the daily budget of campaign "EX I WW I PMAX I SCALING I BROAD"
was raised from 150 to 170 EUR — five weeks after the −55 % cut of 2024-07-16
had produced +45 % vs. account trend. Effect of the re-raise: −22 % (medium
confidence). Even a moderate increase undid part of a fresh, measured win
because it contradicted just-collected evidence.

### 2025-10-23 · PMax Feed-Only Prospecting · +44 % budget amid 8 parallel changes → −31 % (medium)

[Stay Cold · Google Ads · decision evidence · as of 2026-07-03]
On 2025-10-23 the daily budget of campaign "EX I WW I PMAX I SCALING I FEED
ONLY I PROSPECTING" was raised from 90 to 130 EUR. The same step size
(+44 %) had worked on this campaign on 2025-01-23 (+43 % vs. trend, high
confidence, calm window). This time eight other changes ran in parallel and
the effect was −31 % (medium confidence). Same step, different environment,
opposite result — rest windows between changes are not bureaucracy, they are
what makes effects attributable and repeatable.

### 2024-11-21 · auto-apply removed the exact brand keyword before BFCM week

[Stay Cold · Google Ads · decision evidence · as of 2026-07-03]
On 2024-11-21 a Google auto-apply recommendation removed the exact-match
keyword [stay cold] from campaign "EX | DE | SEARCH | BRAND" — two days
before Black Friday week. Verdict negative (formally lower confidence because
it sits in the BFCM window with many parallel changes; substantively one of
the clearest errors of the two years). Across 2024–2026 all 8 auto-apply
clusters on this account touched brand campaigns, with a hit rate of only
~38 % positive. Keyword/asset auto-apply has been assessed as net harmful for
this account.

### 2025-01-23 and 2025-02-20 · brand-exclusion list applied as a batch → mixed to negative

[Stay Cold · Google Ads · decision evidence · as of 2026-07-03]
On 2025-01-23 the negative-keyword list "EXCLUSIONS FOR BRAND" was applied
across brand campaigns in one batch: "EX | FRA | SEARCH | BRAND" −32 % vs.
account trend (high confidence) while two other brand campaigns showed +30 %
and +24 % (medium). On 2025-02-20 the same list was applied to
"EX I SHOPPING I FOKUSPRODUKTE": −23 % (medium) — structurally intended
(brand purchases pushed out of the Shopping campaign), but expensive in that
campaign's own ROAS and introduced without a per-market measurement plan.
Lesson: list rollouts behave differently per market and per channel; apply
one campaign at a time with a measurement window.

## Package events and incidents (context)

### 2025-11-23 · BFCM package — strong result, deliberately not attributed

[Stay Cold · Google Ads · decision evidence · as of 2026-07-03]
On 2025-11-23, two days before Black Friday week, a package of more than 100
changes went live in one day (17 ad changes, 5 budget changes, a
conversion-goal change, audience updates). BFCM 2025 delivered +62 % purchase
revenue vs. BFCM 2024, measurement-clean — but individual attribution inside
a 100-change day is impossible by design, and the analysis marks those
clusters "unclear" instead of guessing. Planned, documented exception
packages are the CORRECT evaluation unit for sale events (per the promo-
calendar guardrail); the goal-change part (NewCustomerPurchase into the
bidding goal) had lasting measurement side effects described in the
measurement-regimes corpus.

### 2026-06-19 to 2026-06-30 · Fokusprodukte saturation endgame — marginal ROAS 0.63, then the cut

[Stay Cold · Google Ads · decision evidence · as of 2026-07-03]
In June 2026 campaign "EX I SHOPPING I FOKUSPRODUKTE" (39 % of H1-2026
account spend) was pushed to ~5,500–11,100 EUR per week while budget-lost
impression share was 0–1 %: weekly clean ROAS fell to 1.5–1.8 and the
marginal clean ROAS of the May→June budget increment was 0.63 — every
additional euro bought 63 cents of purchase revenue, before margin. On
2026-06-30 the daily budget was cut 1,600 → 751 → 550 EUR with the campaign
at a weekly clean ROAS of 0.77. The cut was right, roughly four weeks late.
Contrast: in Feb–Apr 2026 the same campaign scaled acceptably in steps while
budget-lost impression share was 52–67 % (a real limit).

### 2026-03-13 to 2026-03-18 · BS DEALS during Black Spring Sale — deliberate intraday scaling, not evaluable per-change

[Stay Cold · Google Ads · decision evidence · as of 2026-07-10]
During the Black Spring Sale 2026 (documented promo window 2026-03-12 to
2026-03-20), Demand-Gen campaign "BS DEALS - DG_ CP+PDP_S+V" was steered with
deliberate intraday scaling: 2026-03-13 bidding switched target-ROAS →
target-CPA and budget 350 → 525 → 750 EUR; 2026-03-14 up to 1,950 → 2,450 EUR
with tCPA 15 → 20 EUR; 2026-03-16/17 stepped back to 750 → 385 EUR;
2026-03-18 paused. Operator review (2026-07-10) clarified these jumps were
intentional sale tactics, not panic. The per-change 28-day verdicts measured
during this window (−35 % to +32 %) are NOT meaningful for intraday tactics
and are excluded from rule evidence. The correct evaluation unit for such a
window is the promo as a package versus a comparable promo on the
purchase-only series; an intraday tactic would additionally need day/hour
granularity, which this analysis does not have. The campaign itself was
healthy (H1-2026 clean ROAS 5.78).

### 2026-04-27 to 2026-05-24 · "Video View DRAFT" — 4,750 EUR unnoticed for 2 purchases

[Stay Cold · Google Ads · decision evidence · as of 2026-07-03]
Campaign "Video View DRAFT" ran from 2026-04-27 to 2026-05-24, peaking at
1,869 EUR per week, and spent 4,750 EUR for 2 purchases (clean ROAS 0.04). No
change-history entry matches its creation; the name and pattern suggest a
draft/test that went live unintentionally. It ran four weeks unnoticed
because no alert exists for spend without conversions. This is a process
gap, not an analysis error — encoded as a guardrail (alert above 1,000 EUR
cumulative spend without a purchase).
