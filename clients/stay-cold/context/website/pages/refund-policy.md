# Refund Policy

**URL:** https://www.staycoldapparel.com/policies/refund-policy
**Fetched:** 2026-08-03

## Content

### Key timeframes

**14-day statutory right of withdrawal (EU)**
- Consumers have 14 days from receipt to withdraw without giving a reason
- Applies from the day goods are received (or the last item if delivered separately)

**30-day voluntary return policy**
Advertised site-wide as "30 Days Return Policy" / "30 Days Easy Returns" on top of the statutory right.

### Contact for withdrawals
MASCANI GmbH, Kolonnenstr. 8, 10827 Berlin, Germany
Email: support@staycoldapparel.com
Withdrawal form: https://www.staycoldapparel.com/pages/widerruf-formular
Methods: written notice (post/email) or the online withdrawal form

### Refund terms
- Full reimbursement of all payments including standard delivery costs (excluding upgraded shipping)
- Refund issued within 14 days of withdrawal notification
- Same payment method as the original transaction
- No fees charged for reimbursement
- Refund may be withheld until goods are received or return proof is provided

### Consumer responsibilities
- Return goods within 14 days of notifying withdrawal
- Bear direct return shipping costs
- Liable only for value loss from handling beyond what is needed to check condition

### Exclusions
Right of withdrawal does not apply to custom/individualised goods or prefabricated items made to personal specification.

### Ad-copy usable claims
✅ "30 Days Return Policy" — safe, matches site
✅ "Free returns"? — **No.** The customer bears direct return shipping costs. Do not claim free returns.
