### Key findings

- **Unit economics closed.** Gross margin **~75 %** (Airtable `Function: Price Recommender`, "buying price ×4" on DDP incl. VAT — a ×4 markup is mathematically exactly 75 %). Return rate **~5 %**, not the 0.6 % carried in the Notion KB. AOV **~€143** derived from the purchase-only series.
- **Break-even clean ROAS ≈ 1.9** (1.6 if Google receives net values — unverified, gap G1).
- **Two thirds of spend produces roughly zero contribution.** FOKUSPRODUKTE clean ROAS 1.73, PMax PROSPECTING 1.96 — combined 67 % of spend at 1.85, below break-even.
- **Root cause: tROAS targets are set in the inflated currency.** NCP inflation verified still live, ×1.50 account-wide but **×1.28–1.94 per campaign**. FOKUSPRODUKTE's nominal 3.53 is an effective clean target of 1.95; PROSPECTING's 3.60 is 1.92. Search TOF records zero NCP conversions, so its 5.19 means 5.19 — nominal targets are not comparable across campaigns. Worst calibrated: DSA at effective clean 1.29.
- **Account-level recovery is real but uneven.** Spend halved June → July (€61,961 → €30,875), clean ROAS 5.04 → 8.26. The average is carried by brand (11.5 % of spend, 77 % of tracked purchase value).
- **Stay Cold built its own Google Ads reporting layer** (Supabase Class C, live 2026-07-31, `marts.mart_gads__weekly/__monthly`, exposed to their Slack agents). If those marts read `metrics.conversions_value`, their dashboard shows 12.4 where the truth is 8.26 — a numbers conflict waiting to happen.
- **Google is still not defined as a channel** in their Marketing Cheat Sheet; v0.2 has sat in Draft since 2026-06-25 with Google's role marked "Not ready." Owner is now Jonas Makki.
- **Promo calendar conflict.** Drop Base plans a VIP Sale Weekend (2026-10-02..04) and 3For2 Hoodies (2026-12-02..17), which do not fit the locked offer rule "discounts only on Black Friday, only on slow movers." BFCM 2026 exists only as "Proposed by Jonas" (2026-11-23..30), unconfirmed 16 weeks out.
- **New structural discoveries:** a fifth brand campaign (`EX | ESP | SEARCH | BRAND`, €0 spend); exactly two shared negative sets (`EX I ALL` 78 keywords, `EXCLUSIONS FOR BRAND` 3,135); `EXCLUSIONS FOR BRAND` is still attached to FOKUSPRODUKTE despite measuring −23 % there in Feb 2025.
