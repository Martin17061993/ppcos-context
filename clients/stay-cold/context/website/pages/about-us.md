"My mother pushed me into art from the very beginning. I always had this inner ambition that drives me to do creative things."

The company aims to "reach as many people as we can with the brand's message to encourage like-minded people to do their thing, go their own way, and live a free life based on their own rules and values."

Core philosophy: "Because it's your life, and you set the rules, and you have to make the best out of it."
